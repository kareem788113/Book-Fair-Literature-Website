How We've Improved Based on Your Feedback

Mobile-Friendly Website

We've completely redesigned our website to be fully responsive and mobile-friendly, making it easier to browse books and register for events on any device.

More Author Q&A Sessions

Based on popular demand, we've doubled the number of author Q&A sessions and extended their duration to allow for more interaction with attendees.

Enhanced Children's Area

We've expanded our children's programming with more interactive storytelling sessions, craft activities, and a dedicated space for young readers.

Better Food Options

We've partnered with local restaurants to provide a wider variety of food options, including vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free choices.
