Recommendations for Fantasy Books Similar to Lord of the Rings?

Sarah Johnson May 20, 2025 at 10:30 AM Book Discussions

I just finished reading The Lord of the Rings trilogy for the third time, and I'm looking for similar epic fantasy series. I've already read The Wheel of Time and A Song of Ice and Fire. Any recommendations for other immersive fantasy worlds with rich lore and complex characters?

2 replies
Michael Smith May 20, 2025 at 11:45 AM

I'd highly recommend "The First Law" trilogy by Joe Abercrombie. It has morally complex characters and a gritty, realistic take on fantasy. Another great option is "The Stormlight Archive" by Brandon Sanderson - incredible world-building and magic system!

Emily Davis May 20, 2025 at 2:15 PM

If you haven't read it yet, "The Broken Earth" trilogy by N.K. Jemisin is absolutely fantastic. It won the Hugo Award three years in a row! It's not traditional fantasy like LOTR, but the world-building is incredible and the characters are so well developed.
