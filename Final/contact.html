Frequently Asked Questions

When is the next Elite Readers event?

The next Elite Readers Book Fair & Literature Festival is scheduled for September 15-18, 2025. Please check our Events page for more details and updates.

How can I participate as an author?

Authors interested in participating in Elite Readers can apply through our Author Registration form. We welcome both established and emerging authors across all genres. The selection committee reviews applications based on various criteria including relevance to our annual theme.

Are there opportunities for publishers and vendors?

Yes, we offer booth spaces for publishers, bookstores, and literary-related vendors. Please contact us at vendors@elitereaders.com for information about pricing, booth sizes, and availability. Early registration is recommended as spaces fill up quickly.

How can I volunteer at Elite Readers?

We welcome volunteers who are passionate about literature and events. Volunteers can help with event setup, author assistance, children's programming, and more. Please fill out our Volunteer Application form to express your interest and availability.

Is Elite Readers suitable for children?

Absolutely! Elite Readers includes a dedicated Children's Corner with age-appropriate books, storytelling sessions, and interactive activities. We believe in fostering a love for reading from an early age and design our programming to be family-friendly.
