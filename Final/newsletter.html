Frequently Asked Questions

How often will I receive the newsletter?

You can choose your preferred frequency during subscription: weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly. You can change this preference at any time by updating your subscription settings.

Can I customize the content I receive?

Yes! When you subscribe, you can select your topics of interest. We'll tailor the content of your newsletter based on these preferences. You can update these preferences at any time.

How do I unsubscribe if I no longer want to receive the newsletter?

You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any newsletter email. You can also contact our support team at support@litfest.com for assistance.

Is my email address shared with third parties?

No, we respect your privacy. Your email address and personal information will never be shared with third parties without your explicit consent. Please refer to our Privacy Policy for more details.

I'm not receiving the newsletter. What should I do?

First, check your spam or promotions folder. If you still can't find it, please contact us at support@litfest.com, and we'll help troubleshoot the issue. You might need to add our email address to your safe senders list.
