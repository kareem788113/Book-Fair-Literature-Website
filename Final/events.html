Elite Readers Literary Festival 2025

Central Park, New York

10:00 AM - 9:00 PM Daily

Our flagship annual festival brings together the literary world's most celebrated voices for a week of inspiration, discussion, and celebration. This year's festival explores the theme "Reimagining Tomorrow" with a focus on speculative fiction, futurism, and visionary writing.

Event Highlights:

  • Over 50 author readings and book signings
  • Interactive workshops for aspiring writers
  • Children's literature corner with activities for young readers
  • Poetry slams and open mic sessions
  • Book marketplace featuring independent publishers

Daily Themes:

  • June 20: Science Fiction & Fantasy
  • June 21: Literary Fiction & Poetry
  • June 22: Mystery, Thriller & Crime
  • June 23: Historical Fiction & Non-Fiction
  • June 24: Young Adult & Children's Literature
  • June 25: Global Voices & Translated Works

International Authors Summit 2025

British Library, London

8:30 AM - 6:00 PM

A professional conference for authors, publishers, and literary agents focusing on the business and craft of writing in today's rapidly evolving publishing landscape. This year's summit addresses the impact of AI on writing and publishing.

Conference Tracks:

  • The Business of Writing: Contracts, Rights & Royalties
  • Digital Publishing & Marketing Strategies
  • Craft Masterclasses with Award-Winning Authors
  • AI & Technology in Publishing
  • Global Markets & Translation Opportunities

Registration Options:

  • Full Conference Pass: $495 (Early Bird: $395)
  • One-Day Pass: $195
  • Virtual Attendance: $250
  • Student/Emerging Author: $195
